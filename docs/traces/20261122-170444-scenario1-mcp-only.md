### Final answer
Extra internet costs **$10 per additional 10 GB block** beyond the 20 GB included allowance. A partially used block is charged as a full block (Billing Guide §3.2, §8.1).

For example:
- 20 GB: $50
- 21 GB: $60
- 30 GB: $60
- 35 GB: $70
